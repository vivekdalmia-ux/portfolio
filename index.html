Overview

Engineering & Background

I am a Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence (S&AI) student at Newton School of Technology (NST). My work focuses on building full-stack platforms that bridge complex backends with high-performance interactive interfaces.

My flagship developments include Rivaat (a 3D doorstep trial commerce platform on React 19 & Three.js), BitForge (a multi-chain crypto custodial vault with social tipping bot capabilities), and Mann 2.0 (an AI-powered hostel operating system that parses unstructured student chat dumps into prioritized maintenance actions).
